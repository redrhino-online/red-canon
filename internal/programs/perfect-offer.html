Offer path · choose the result

  1. List the pains and results you can change.
  2. Rank them by urgency and clear proof.
  3. Ask buyers to choose if two results remain.

Check: buyers already seek this important, measurable result. Output: chosen currency and buyer evidence. Handoff: define the customer next. If uncertain, ask more buyers; do not build around a guess.

Offer path · shape the promise

  1. Name a reachable group with the shared problem. Check where they gather and what they already try.
  2. Write one message: who, result, measure and time, without the main pain or barrier.
  3. Test the wording with likely buyers. Keep their questions and phrases; remove vague terms.

Check: buyers understand the result and say it matters now. Output and handoff: customer profile and tested message guide the roadmap. If unclear, narrow the result, use buyer words, and ask again.

Offer path · build the route

  1. Dump knowledge and market findings before sorting.
  2. Group required work into three stages and nine ordered steps.
  3. Add three actions per step. Remove anything not needed for the result.

Check: every step is essential, in order, and has a deliverable. Mark the weakest link. If the route is unclear, combine or cut steps that do not serve the result.

Offer path · buyer test

  1. Set scope, support, proof, price, and next action on one page.
  2. Show it to ten likely buyers. Ask them to repeat the promise and next step.
  3. Record questions, revise the unclear parts, and test again.

Check: buyers can repeat the promise and name the next action. If not, narrow the result and retest before building.

Offer path · hand off and finish

Output: a tested one-page offer and product roadmap.

Handoff: give the roadmap to the people building delivery and sales materials.

End result: one clear route from a current problem to a measurable result.
