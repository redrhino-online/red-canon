14-day path · prepare and choose

Before day 1: set the launch date, reserve daily work time, gather customer questions, and check recording and payment tools. If access to buyers or proof is missing, line up a small pilot.

  1. Day 1 · Currency. List results. Choose one urgent, measurable result. Ask buyers if unclear.
  2. Day 2 · Customer. Name a reachable group and what they already try. Broaden if you cannot reach them.

Checkpoint: one result and reachable group are clear. Output: currency and customer notes.

14-day path · test and gather

  1. Day 3 · Message. Write who, result, measure, time, and barrier removed. Test with three buyers; revise unclear words.
  2. Day 4 · Brain dump. List topics, questions, and possible steps without sorting. If stuck, review market examples.

Checkpoint: buyers understand and want the promise. Output: tested message and unsorted knowledge list.

14-day path · set the route

Day 5 · Roadmap. Group essential work into three stages and nine ordered steps. Remove or combine steps that do not lead to the result.

Handoff: the tested message and roadmap set the course outline. If unclear, return to the buyer result and reorder the steps.

14-day path · outline and draft

  1. Day 6 · Outline. Make one module per step, with promise, lesson order, and close.
  2. Day 7 · Lesson sheets. Add goal, struggle, result, example, actions, and homework to each step.
  3. Day 8 · Fill gaps. Check facts; research missing points and write original explanations.

Checkpoint: each lesson has one clear task and learner tool. Output and handoff: nine lesson sheets move into slide design. If a gap remains, research that point only; do not expand the whole course.

14-day path · design and price

  1. Day 9 · Slides. Use one slide pattern, one idea per slide, short text, and a useful example.
  2. Day 10 · Price. Set a premium price from the result and support. If buyers resist, review proof and promise before price.
  3. Day 11 · Review. Compare every lesson with the roadmap. Ask a learner to try one; repair any unclear task.

Checkpoint and handoff: the learner can follow each lesson and do its task. Output: reviewed slides, learner tools, and price for the production phase.

14-day path · production plan

  1. Day 12 · Plan. Batch lessons, name files, and choose a simple recording path.
  2. Day 13 · Record. Record one lesson or slide section at a time. Fix clear errors and upload each module.

Output: recorded lessons in order. If a tool blocks progress, switch to a simpler path.

14-day path · test and launch

Day 14. Test payment, access, and the first lesson as a learner. Fix failures, invite buyers, and log enrollments and questions.

Gate: a learner can pay, find, open, and complete lessons in order. If time slips, launch the smallest complete path and schedule polish after release.

End result: a live premium course with a clear offer and tested first learner path.
