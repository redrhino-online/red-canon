Q: Why did you create this platform?

Great question! We built LaunchMAPS to solve a major problem in the digital marketing space: Most entrepreneurs and marketers struggle or fail because there was no clear path or support system for launching an online business. Available options were limited to 1,000's of courses and information products that only solved one piece of the marketing puzzle, business coaching programs that lacked 1:1 support and agencies that can only help if you have an offer that converts. So we created a new model that allows us to provide intensive 1:1 support for a lower investment than most coaches charge.

Q: How do I know if this will work for me?

This platform is ideal for coaches, consultants (including agencies) and course creators who have an existing business that solves an important problem for clients, are willing to charge $3K or more and (with our coaching) can enroll at least 2 out of 5 qualified prospects they talk to.

How about joining the super Inner Circle Elite Top Secret Masterminds®? This is like being dropped in the middle of a massive junkyard and being asked to assemble a running automobile. We provide you with a complete and proven "A-Z" system that you can actually follow, combined with world-class support to help you get there.

Q: Why do I have to charge $3K or more for my program/product?

Very important question! Selling low priced products is actually MORE DIFFICULT when you really look at the metrics. Launching books, courses and other lower ticket products makes sense only after you have in place a core program that's generating significant sales on a weekly basis.
