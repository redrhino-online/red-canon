# Engage Opportunity

Engage Opportunity is one of the three phases of the RED Method. Here we turn
interest into a paying client. The phase has three motions: Funnel, Traffic, and Enroll.
Each motion has three steps.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[Funnel] --> B[Traffic] --> C[Enroll]
```

## The Funnel motion

We build the simple path that turns a stranger into a booked call. Keep it
short. Friction is the enemy.

1. **Script the authority video.** We make a short video that shows the
   signature solution. We use a six-part script: promise, proof, problems,
   steps, context, action.
2. **Build the four pages.** We build four pages: opt in, video, calendar, and
   confirmation. We add a second click before the video to raise quality.
3. **Brand, record, edit.** We add branding, slides, recording, and editing. We
   add a homework video so people show up ready.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[Opt in] --> B[Video] --> C[Calendar] --> D[Client]
```

You leave with a live funnel and a finished authority video.

### The lead magnet

We do not invent a lead magnet. We pull one hot step from your own offer and
turn it into a one-page cheat sheet. Then every step of your offer can become
its own lead magnet. This is called the wheel of awesome.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[One step] --> B[One page] --> C[New lead]
```

## The Traffic motion

We turn on paid ads the right way. We also bring back the people who got stuck.

1. **Set up tracking.** We set up tracking, audiences, and goals.
2. **Launch the campaign.** We build a simple campaign: one campaign, one ad
   set, a few ads. Then we launch and leave it alone for about 10 days.
3. **Retarget and tune one thing at a time.** We split audiences by where they
   stopped. We show a focused ad to each group. We watch a few key numbers and
   change only one thing at a time.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[Setup] --> B[Launch] --> C[Retarget] --> D[Fix one thing]
```

You leave with a live ad campaign, a retargeting plan, and a metrics dashboard.
See [how the numbers work](numbers.md).

## The Enroll motion

We run the enrollment call. We help a booked lead decide, with no pressure.

1. **Frame the call.** We set the time, the purpose, and the outcome. We ask why
   they booked and if they want help now. We check intent before we go on.
2. **Diagnose the gap.** We audit the lead against your product roadmap. We ask
   for exact numbers. We do not solve or pitch yet. We check commitment.
3. **Invite and handle objections.** We give a short plan. We answer doubts with
   questions, not pressure. Then we invite them to be the next client.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[Frame] --> B[Diagnose] --> C[Invite]
```

### The checkpoints

A checkpoint is a quick gate inside the call. The lead must pass it before you
move on. The gates are intent, commitment, clarity, confidence, and desire.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[Intent] --> B[Commitment] --> C[Clarity] --> D[Confidence] --> E[Desire]
```

If a gate fails, we stop or step back. We do not push. A short, honest call is a
win. We clear the doubts before the close, so the lead does not raise a new one
at the end.

We design and implement all of this for you. We write the words, the questions,
and the checkpoints. We practice the call with you until it feels natural, not
pushy.

### Three ways to enroll

We pick the model that fits the offer.

- **One call.** An application gates the call. One strong conversation.
- **Two calls.** A short triage call, then the main call.
- **A paid session.** The lead pays for a roadmap session. It qualifies and it
  earns revenue.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[One call] --> B[Two calls] --> C[Paid session]
```

## The enrollment evolution

We enroll clients in two paths. The simple path is a lead magnet to a call. The
scaled path adds a webinar: lead to webinar to call.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[Lead magnet] --> B[Call]
    C[Lead] --> D[Webinar] --> E[Call]
```

Build the simple path first. Improve it until it works. Then add the webinar.
The webinar is a second enrollment mechanism. It teaches and sells at scale.
