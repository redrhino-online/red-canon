Rule 1: Be an advisor

Not a vendor. A vendor competes on price. An advisor leads the work.

You drive the process. Every time.

Rule 2: Price on outcome

Charge for the result, not the hours.

  • No price cuts without removing work.
  • Value the solution, not the time.

Rule 3: Solve one critical problem

One clear problem. One fixed time frame.

Never sell a vague service.

Rule 4: Shrink the problem

No proof yet? Offer a smaller piece first.

  • Build their lead magnet.
  • Run a short workshop.
  • Then grow the work.

Rule 5: Answer with context

People need context, not more content.

Every question lives inside your roadmap.
