Learning program

14-day course launch

Go from a rough idea to a live premium course in 14 days.

Swipe or use the arrow keys. The deck runs in its own tab.

The promise

One clear outcome each day. Fourteen days. One live product.

Action beats perfection.

A premium course

A course that solves an important problem and commands a premium price.

  • Simple, specific outcome.
  • Fixed time frame.
  • Clear path from A to B.

Day 1: Choose a currency

One measurable thing you change. One word.

If they missed it, they would be upset.

Day 2: The perfect customer

Name one group. Not everyone.

  • What do they want?
  • What holds them back?
  • Where do they gather?

Day 3: Craft the message

One sentence. Who, result, time, without.

I help ___ get ___ in ___ without ___.

Day 4: The digital brain dump

Write every idea on the topic. Do not sort yet.

A messy list first. Order comes later.

Day 5: The product roadmap

Sort the dump into three stages. Then three steps each.

Stage 1
Stage 2
Stage 3

Three stages. Nine steps.

Why three by three

  • Easy to remember.
  • Easy to teach.
  • Easy to sell.

People hold three things in mind with ease.

Day 6: Course outline

Turn each step into a module. Write the order.

  • A short intro.
  • One lesson per step.
  • A close with the next step.

Day 7: Content crushers

One sheet per step. Fill it by hand.

On each sheet

Goal, pain, steps, example, action.

Why sheets

They become slides and scripts.

Practice

Fill one crusher

Pick step one. Fill the sheet. Keep it short.

Day 8: Answer the gaps

Went blank? Look at how others teach the topic.

Use their shape. Keep your own words.

Day 9: The slide template

One template repeats across every module.

  • Title and promise.
  • Teach slides.
  • Example slide.
  • Action slide.

Slide rules

  • One idea per slide.
  • Short text. No long lists.
  • One image with meaning.

Day 10: Price the product

Charge for the result. Set one premium price.

Do

Hold the price. Add value.

Do not

Cut the price fast.

Day 11: Slide coaching

Check the deck against the script.

  • Does each slide match its step?
  • Is the text short?
  • Is the brand the same?

Day 12: The production plan

Plan the recording before you record.

Batch

Day 13: Record and edit fast

Done is better than perfect.

  • Use one take per slide.
  • Skip heavy editing.
  • Ship the module.

Marketing built in

Your course is your marketing.

  • Each step is a video.
  • Each step is a post.
  • Each step is a lead magnet.

Build down. Scale up.

Launch the premium offer first. Add smaller offers later.

Do not start low to build courage.

Make it stick
