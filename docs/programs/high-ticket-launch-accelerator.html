Phase 9: Enroll

The calm call

You are not a used car salesperson. You are a doctor.

The call has a check, a plan, and an invitation.

Six parts of the call

  1. Frame: set the rules.
  2. Discover: find the gaps.
  3. Problems: name the cost.
  4. Prescribe: give the plan.
  5. Apply: check the fit.
  6. Invite: offer the seat.

Audit against the roadmap

Ask about each step of your roadmap. Find where they are stuck.

Do not solve on the call. Just find the gaps.

If you are talking, you are losing.

Checkpoints keep it honest

Each part ends with a pass or fail question.

  • Are they looking for help now?
  • How committed are they, one to five?
  • How confident are they in the plan?

No passing grade? Go back a step. Do not push on.

Practice

Run the six parts

Say the six parts out loud. Time yourself.

Short calls win. Aim for the shortest useful call.

Common mistakes

  • Building a low price first.
  • Making a puzzle of many funnels.
  • Chasing tactics before the offer.
  • Solving on the call too early.
