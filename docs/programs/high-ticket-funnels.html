The 1% rule

Plan for the worst. About 1% of leads buy.

  • 3 to 5% book a call.
  • 20 to 40% of calls enroll.
  • One price that makes the math work.

Do not celebrate cheap leads

A lead is only good if it can become a client.

Leads mean nothing until they buy.
