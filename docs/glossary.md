# Glossary

- **1% rule**: the planning rule that about one lead in one hundred buys. If it
  works at 1%, it works at any higher rate.
- **Application**: a short form a lead fills in before a call. It filters and
  gives you their numbers.
- **Asset**: something that keeps working after you build it.
- **Audience**: the group of people who know you and follow you.
- **Avatar**: the one person you serve.
- **Avatar Goals Grid**: a chart of what your one person wants. It lists their
  goals, pains, and fears.
- **Authority Amplifier**: another name for the authority video. A short video
  that shows your offer and builds trust.
- **Authority Amplifier blocks**: the six-part video script: promise, proof,
  problems, steps, context, and action. The same six blocks run ads, emails, and
  sales letters.
- **Authority video**: a short video that shows your offer and builds trust. It
  turns a stranger into a fan.
- **Booking window**: the time between a lead booking and the call. Best within
  72 hours. Ideal is 24 to 48 hours.
- **Branding Images**: the pictures that match your brand. They go on your
  slides and pages.
- **Build line**: the 12-week program that builds the machine. It ends when the
  run line starts.
- **Checklist**: a one-page tick list for recurring work.
- **Checkpoint**: a quick gate inside a sales call. The lead must pass it before
  you move on.
- **Client Engine**: the first output of the RED Method engagement and the
  first asset in the client's RED Portfolio: the five stages of Build,
  Launch, Serve, Grow, and Partner.
- **Closing sequence**: the emails and ads that run for a few days after a
  webinar. It drives most of the sales.
- **Content Crusher**: a one-page sheet that captures one step of the offer. It
  holds the promise, the pain, the goal, the story, the steps, and the action.
- **Cost per client**: the ad cost to win one client.
- **Cost per lead**: the ad cost to get one lead.
- **Currency**: the one measured result you sell.
- **Currency Calculator**: a tool that helps you pick one result to sell. It
  has two columns: what you increase and what you decrease.
- **Delivery ladder**: the three ways to deliver: one to one, a live cohort,
  then a recorded evergreen program.
- **Digital brain dump**: the step where you list every topic the buyer must
  learn, before you shape the product.
- **Enrollment call**: the call where a booked lead decides to become a client.
- **Enrollment evolution**: the two paths of enrollment. The simple path is a
  lead magnet to a call. The scaled path adds a webinar: lead to webinar to
  call.
- **Extract**: the first motion of Develop Audience. We pull key ideas from the
  signature solution and build an email and social plan.
- **Five Ps (5P)**: the five kinds of message: problem, promise, proof, ping
  (a question), and promotion.
- **Floodgates**: the ways you get people to your site. You can pay for ads,
  post content, or retarget.
- **Four Levels**: the four groups in your market, split by how much success
  they have. You pick which groups you serve.
- **Funnel**: the steps that turn a stranger into a booked call.
- **Funnel math**: the chain of numbers from spend to leads to calls to
  clients.
- **Gate**: a check you pass before moving to the next step.
- **Hot and cold currencies**: hot currencies are money, health, love, and
  business results. Cold words like growth and authority do not sell.
- **Hub and spoke**: many small pieces of content feed one center. The center
  can be a group or a webinar.
- **IP**: the ideas and materials you own.
- **Launch Accelerator**: the training program. It walks you through the whole
  build.
- **Launch Map**: one page that shows the whole plan. It puts every step in one
  place.
- **Lead**: a person who gave you their contact details.
- **Lead-value curve**: a lead is worth a little on day one and more over time.
  Most leads buy later.
- **Lifetime value**: what one client is worth over time. It is the master
  number of the business.
- **Mastermind**: a group of business owners who help each other. They meet to
  share advice and support.
- **Metric**: a number you watch to judge progress.
- **Metrics chain**: the numbers from spend to leads to calls to clients. Each
  step has one number.
- **Million Dollar Message**: one clear sentence about what you do. It names the
  person, the result, and the time.
- **Mini asset**: a template, checklist, calculator, or video set for one step.
- **Mini offer**: a paid audit, review, or done-for-you service for one step.
- **Mini product**: a short course, tool, or workshop that solves one step.
- **Money message**: the plain name for the million dollar message. See
  [the money message](method/money-message.md).
- **Motion**: a group of activities inside a phase. There are nine motions,
  three in each phase.
- **Objection**: a doubt a lead raises before they decide.
- **Objection Crusher**: the way we answer a buyer's doubt. We ask questions
  instead of pushing.
- **Offer**: the product you sell and the price.
- **Paid strategy session**: a session a lead pays for. They get a plan for
  their next 90 days. It qualifies the lead and earns revenue.
- **Perfect Offer Map**: a tool that helps you shape your offer and message. It
  makes both stronger.
- **Perfect Product**: the training on how to package your work. It turns what
  you know into a product.
- **Perfect Sales Script**: the full training for the sales script. It teaches
  the steps, the questions, and the answers to doubts.
- **Phase**: one of the three parts of the RED Method.
- **Playbook**: a guide for one station or service line. It gives the goal, the
  steps, the gate, and the metrics.
- **Pre-call homework**: a short task a lead does before the call. It moves the
  free line and filters the lead.
- **Product ladder**: the offer sliced into steps, phases, and the whole
  program. Each slice is a product.
- **Product roadmap**: the map of the offer. It has three stages and nine steps.
  See [the product roadmap](method/product-roadmap.md).
- **Profit Pyramid**: a picture of your market in four levels. It shows who you
  serve and who you do not.
- **RED Method**: our method. Three phases: Refine Offer, Engage Opportunity,
  Develop Audience. Nine motions. 27 steps.
- **RED Portfolio**: the stack of IP assets built in our work together. The
  Client Engine is the first one. The Grow stage adds more. Assets in the RED
  Portfolio are licensed to you for use under your agreement with us.
- **Retargeting**: showing ads to people who already visited. It is part of the
  Traffic motion.
- **Retargeting Roadmap**: the plan for showing ads to people who visited. It
  brings them back.
- **Roadmap audit**: checking a client against their own roadmap to find the
  missing step. We run it about every 90 days.
- **Rule of threes**: the shape of the roadmap: three stages, nine steps,
  twenty-seven actions.
- **Run line**: the ongoing work after the build. Paid ads, content, and
  retargeting run all the time.
- **Sales script**: the steps and questions we use on the enrollment call.
- **Signature solution**: the visual path from a buyer's pain to their goal. It
  has three phases and nine steps.
- **SOP**: a precise procedure for one repeatable task.
- **Stage**: how the work shows up over time: Build, Launch, Serve, Grow, and
  Partner.
- **Station**: one unit of the build line. There are nine stations. Each station
  maps to one or more of the 27 steps.
- **Step**: one action inside a motion. There are 27 steps.
- **Swim Lanes**: the paths people take through your funnel. Each lane is a step
  toward a customer.
- **Ten-and-ten rule**: automate a webinar only after ten live runs at 10% or
  better.
- **The three C's**: the three things a buyer needs: commitment, clarity, and
  confidence.
- **Three enrollment models**: the three ways to enroll: one call, two calls, or
  a paid session.
- **Two-step opt in**: a second click before the video. It raises lead quality.
- **Weakest-link test**: if a step is removed and the result still stands, the
  step is not needed.
- **Welcome filter**: a simple question in a group that finds the people who
  want help.
- **Wheel of awesome**: every step of your offer can become a lead magnet. Never
  build one from scratch.
- **Winning Webinar**: a talk that teaches and sells. It gives value first, then
  makes an offer. It is a second enrollment mechanism, used in the scaled path
  of the enrollment evolution.
