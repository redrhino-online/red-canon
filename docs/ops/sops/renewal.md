# Renewal SOP

This procedure keeps a client for another term.

## Trigger
The client's term is 60 days from its end. Or the client's results are strong and the next step is clear.

## Roles
The partnership lead runs this procedure. The delivery lead gives the results. The coach gives progress notes.

## Procedure
1. Pull the [client scorecard](../checklists/client-scorecard.md). Check attendance, progress, and results.
2. Run a health check. Ask the client how the work feels.
3. Review the results against the goals from kickoff.
4. Pick the next step. Match it to the client's new goal.
5. Build a simple offer for the next term.
6. Share the offer on a call. Do not rush.
7. Answer questions. Ask for a clear yes or no.
8. Close the renewal. Set the new start date.
9. Send a short thank-you note.

Here is the renewal path.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[Health check] --> B[Review results] --> C[Offer next step] --> D[Close]
```

## Decision points
- If results are strong, offer the next step.
- If results are weak, fix the work first. Do not offer yet.
- If the client is quiet, run a win-back talk.
- If the client says no, ask why. Save the reason.

## Escalation
Tell the delivery lead when results are weak. Tell the partnership lead when a client is at risk. Tell the owner when a large client may leave.

## Records
Save the scorecard, the results review, the offer, and the signed renewal. Keep them in the client folder.
